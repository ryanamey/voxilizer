    Prototype source-filter synthesis: a modelled glottal pulse plus added noise and sub-harmonic sources, shaped by five formant resonances. With your own voice, the take is split by linear prediction into a source and your vowel filter; effects are added to the source in time with your pitch, then your filter is put back on. Recordings are processed in this browser and never uploaded. The sound is an approximation for choosing a target. It does not show how the throat should feel, so never push to copy it. Mode names follow Complete Vocal Technique terminology; stage rules are prototype heuristics for coaches to refine.
